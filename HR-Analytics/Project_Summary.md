# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Objective
Analyze employee data to identify attrition trends and workforce insights.

## Tools
- Power BI
- SQL
- Excel
- DAX

## KPIs
- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Salary
- Average Performance

## Business Insights
- Finance department has the highest attrition.
- Overall attrition rate is 17.4%.
- Average salary is around ₹11 lakh.
